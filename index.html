How a visit works

The Process

  1. 01

    Consultation

    We look at your hair in daylight, check porosity and ask how you style it at home.

    15 min · free
  2. 02

    Strand test

    For color, a skin patch test 48 hours ahead and a test on one strand.

    Color clients only
  3. 03

    The service

    Cut, color, styling or treatment, with your formula saved on your client card.

    45–210 min
  4. 04

    Finish & aftercare

    A finish you can repeat at home and a written note on products and timing.

    Every visit
